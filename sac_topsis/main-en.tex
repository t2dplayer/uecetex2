\abstract{%
Task offloading in Vehicular Edge Computing (\acrotip{VEC}) networks is a critical mechanism to satisfy the stringent latency and energy constraints of advanced autonomous applications. High vehicle mobility creates an environment where the number of available edge servers and neighboring vehicles changes continuously, making the observation space dimensionally variable. This variability prevents standard Deep Reinforcement Learning (\acrotip{DRL}) agents from generalizing across different vehicular densities without costly retraining. To address this challenge, this article proposes \textbf{\acrotip{SAC}-\acrotip{TOPSIS}}, a hybrid decision-making architecture that integrates the Technique for Order of Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution (\acrotip{TOPSIS}) into the Soft Actor-Critic (\acrotip{SAC}) algorithm. The \acrotip{TOPSIS} module evaluates each offloading candidate node, characterized by Job Completion Time (\acrotip{JCT}), energy consumption, and physical distance, using $L_1$-Manhattan distances with weights $[0.4;\, 0.3;\, 0.3]$, and compresses the variable-sized observation into a fixed 6-dimensional, scale-invariant state vector. A \acrotip{SAC} agent receives this compact representation and learns a binary policy: execute locally or offload to the best-ranked candidate. Entropy regularization prevents the policy from collapsing into purely local or purely offloading modes. Extensive simulations in 601 heterogeneous Vehicle-to-Everything (\acrotip{V2X}) and 5G scenarios demonstrate that \acrotip{TOPSIS} integration consistently improves all tested \acrotip{DRL} families: \acrotip{SAC} improves from $4.47\%$ to $44.36\%$ ($+39.9$\,pp), Twin Delayed DDPG (\acrotip{TD3}) from $41.62\%$ to $43.01\%$ ($+1.4$\,pp), and Proximal Policy Optimization (\acrotip{PPO}) from $41.61\%$ to $41.95\%$ ($+0.3$\,pp) in the deadline compliance rate. Among all evaluated policies, \acrotip{SAC}-\acrotip{TOPSIS} achieves the highest compliance rate ($44.36\%$) and the highest Success-Weighted Quality (\acrotip{SWQ}) index ($0.1450$). Fundamentally, the model is trained on a single density regime and generalizes zero-shot to unseen vehicular densities from 10 to \SI{30}{tasks\per\second}.%
}
