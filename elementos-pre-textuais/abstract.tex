This qualification work proposes and evaluates a solution to the task offloading problem in Vehicular Edge Computing (\gls{VEC}) networks by combining multicriteria ranking via \gls{TOPSIS} (\textit{Technique for Order of Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution}) with Deep Reinforcement Learning (\gls{DRL}) based on the Soft Actor-Critic (\gls{SAC}) algorithm. The central challenge is making \textit{offloading} decisions under deadline constraints in environments with a variable number of candidates --- Road Side Units (\glspl{RSU}) and neighboring vehicles --- which prevents the direct use of fixed-input neural networks without retraining. The main technical contribution of this stage is a compact state engineering approach (6D vector) that encapsulates the normalized features of the best candidate identified by \gls{TOPSIS}, guaranteeing scale-invariance and learning stability regardless of vehicle density. \gls{TOPSIS} evaluates multicriteria such as estimated latency, queue occupancy, channel rate, and energy consumption, reducing the complexity of the state space without losing information critical for decision-making. The use of \gls{SAC} is motivated by its maximum-entropy regularization, which prevents policy collapse in non-stationary environments, and by the sample efficiency of \textit{off-policy} learning. Experiments conducted over 658 simulation scenarios with Vehicle-to-Everything (\gls{V2X}) communication show that the SAC-TOPSIS combination achieves a deadline compliance rate of 45.21\%, a \textit{Success-Weighted Quality} (SWQ) of 0.1484, and an objective function value of $J(\pi) = 0.2454$ --- metrics superior to those of five other families of \gls{DRL} algorithms and nine reference heuristics evaluated. As a planned extension, \gls{TOPSIS} will be replaced by \textit{Fuzzy}-TOPSIS, incorporating linguistic uncertainty into the candidate evaluation criteria, with the aim of improving the robustness of state representation in high-mobility and imprecise-information scenarios.

\keywords{task offloading; vehicular edge computing; deep reinforcement learning; SAC; TOPSIS; \textit{Fuzzy}-TOPSIS; V2X; zero-shot generalization; state engineering.}
